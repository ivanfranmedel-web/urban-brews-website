Meet our team!

Elena Marsh

Founder & Head Roaster

Elena started Urban Brews out of a love for single-origin beans and a stubborn belief that Dublin needed better coffee. She sources and roasts every batch in-house, and still can't resist tasting a fresh pour before it goes on the menu.

Tomás Herrera

Store Manager

Tomás keeps the day-to-day running smoothly, from staff rotas to stock orders. He's the one you'll usually see behind the counter first thing in the morning, and he knows most of the regulars by name and order.

Priya Nandakumar

Head Barista

Priya trained as a barista in Melbourne before joining the team, and brought her love of latte art with her. She runs our weekend coffee workshops and is always happy to talk you through the menu if you're not sure what to order.

Liam O'Connor

Pastry Chef

Liam bakes everything in the display case fresh each morning, from the sourdough to the cinnamon rolls. He's big on using local suppliers, and is constantly testing new recipes on the rest of the team before they make the menu.

Aoife Byrne

Community & Events Coordinator

Aoife organises the open mic nights and local artist showcases that run in the shop most months. She's also the person behind Urban Brews' Instagram, so if you've tagged us in a photo, she's probably already seen it.

About Us

Welcome to Urban Brews, your comfiest neighborhood coffee shop where we always serve high-quality, handmade coffee with a side of friendly smiles. Located in Dublin City , Urban Brews is a place where all kind of coffee lovers meet and come together to enjoy not only a lovely brewed coffee but also a sense of community and connection to those who visit.

Our Mission

At Urban Brews, we are passionate about coffee and our coummunity. Our mission is to provide the greatest coffee experience while creating a warm, welcoming space for every customer who walks through our doors. From our naturally sourced irish beans to our signature blends, we strive to make every cup a lasting immpression to each of our customers . Whether you're here for a morning pick-ups, a quick cup before work or a relaxing break, we want Urban Brews to be your go to destination for a great coffee and the best vibes.

Our Story

Founded in 2016 by 5 local friends that had great taste for coffee, Urban Brews began as a small, community oriented café. What started as a big dream has since grown into a beloved spot in the hearth of Dublin city. We strongly believe that coffee is more than just a drink it's a way to connect with others, share stories, have laughs and build memories. Over the years, we’ve expanded our bussiness but our commitment to quality and community remains the same at the heart of everything we do.
